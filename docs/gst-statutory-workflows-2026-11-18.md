## Dated changes that matter for the supported boundary

- **Specified premises, from 1 April 2025.** [05/2025-CT(R)](https://www.gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-01/ctr05-2025.pdf) tests actual supply value, not a declared/advertised tariff. VII and IX are filed January 1–March 31 before the applicable FY. VIII uses the ARN date through ARN +15 days; the effective registration date under [Rule 10](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter3/rule10_v1.00.html) may precede ARN. An opt-in continues unless validly opted out; 2025–26 manual opt-ins had a specific Jan–Mar 2026 electronic filing transition ([Council Jan 2026 newsletter](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-02/newsletter_january_issue_0.pdf)), not a general annual renewal rule. [15/2025-CT(R), Gazette 266220](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/266220.pdf) amends the definition/forms for accommodation and additional-place applications with effect from April 1; its September 22 change to entry 7(i) is accommodation, not a blanket restaurant-rate change. The additional-place/registration-amendment route is not automated; unresolved dates or filings go to owner/adviser review. The statutory date and the app's outlet activation date are separate.
- **Credit notes and discounts.** The [Finance Act 2025, §126](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/262125.pdf) amended the §34(2) proviso (commencement October 1, 2025 under [16/2025-CT, S.O. 4206(E), Gazette copy](https://cd.taxcode.in/Legal+Contents/GST/Notifications/CT/2025/Notification-16-2025-CT-17092025.pdf)); review attributable ITC reversal for a registered recipient and tax incidence in other cases. The [Finance Act 2026, §§153–155](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/271439.pdf) amendments to §§15, 34 and 54 commenced **November 1, 2026** under [03/2026-CT, S.O. 5791(E), Gazette 277441](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/277441.pdf). Qualifying post-supply discounts now use a §34 credit note and applicable recipient ITC reversal under the substituted §15(3)(b), rather than its old pre-agreement and specific-invoice conditions. AutoDine keeps the source link for audit and does **not** turn this change into tax reduction, refund, partial-note or filing logic. Review date, legal basis, earlier filed return and all conditions outside the app. [57th Council recommendations](https://www.gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-10/press_release_0.pdf) were not treated as operative changes without implementing law.
- **IRP wire evidence and limits.** See [NIC INV-01 workbook](https://einvoice1.gst.gov.in/Documents/EINVOICE_SCHEMA.xlsx), [version 1.03 generation](https://einv-apisandbox.nic.in/version1.03/generate-irn.html), [get by document](https://einv-apisandbox.nic.in/version1.03/Get_IRNdetailsbyDocDetails.html), [NIC release notes](https://einv-apisandbox.nic.in/release-notes.html) and [GSTN advisory 592](https://www.gst.gov.in/newsandupdates/read/592). Provider duplicates (including NIC 2150/2295) need lookup by registration, FY, type and document number before any retry; uppercase document numbers are enforced from June 1, 2025. NIC describes `AckNo`, `AckDt`, `ACT`/`CNL` and SHA256RSA signed invoice/QR artifacts. Its raw timestamp and response must be normalized by an authorized adapter with a defined timezone and precise acknowledgment number, not guessed by the test helper. The helper checks signatures against a supplied key, expected document content and typed evidence; this is not tested against a real provider or its master-data/schema versions. [IRIS viewing guide](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/kb/viewing-e-invoices/) shows signed record retrieval, not proof of API credentials or acceptance. Offline shape validation is not certification or final delivery. In this refresh the public NIC by-document and release-notes/cancellation pages did not load reliably; earlier technical observations are not a fresh provider contract. Reconfirm against the exact authorized provider version before integration.

### Evidence-to-code checkpoints

- Rule 46/49 output: `00900`, `01300`, `01600` → `lib/db/invoice.ts`, `InvoiceScreen`, `buildReceiptMessage`; native threshold and missing-particulars refusal and print fixtures. Historical SAC-less BOS remains flagged, even on an old share link. Unregistered retail is a different saved document class, not a GST invoice.
- Rule 53: `01600` → `gst_guard_note_particulars`, `noteParticulars` and private note route; native 12-note suite, seeded predecessor and private print tests. The clock rehearsal uses unchanged issue functions to test both financial-year numbering and the outer November 30 cutoff. None of this checks the taxpayer's earlier return date.
- FSSAI: `lib/db/payment-receipt.ts` displays only format-valid FSSAI from the original embedded seller, independently of later settings; print and text fixture asserts exact ₹105.11. A later payment acknowledgment is not a replacement GST document or additional proof of legal issue.
- PAN/IRP: `01600` keeps readers shared against the exclusive settings writer; `01700` private load and conditional CAS use durable database time and explicit lookup evidence. No artifact verification, printing or release is promoted by that store. Offline signature/content contracts are not regulatory retention, trust-key rotation, certified master data or live acceptance.
