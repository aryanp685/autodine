## When an IRN is needed

- **Ordinary B2C restaurant bills do not go to IRP.** Normal receipts and Composition Bills of Supply are outside this flow. A B2C payment QR, where applicable, is not the signed IRP QR.
- **Covered B2B and exports:** from **1 August 2023**, a non-exempt supplier that exceeded **₹5 crore** in PAN-wide aggregate turnover in any preceding financial year since 2017–18 is generally in scope. ₹5 crore exactly does not meet the exceedance test. The supplier, not the buyer or a single outlet, is tested. Missing evidence does not establish exemption; actual taxpayer facts were not checked. See [Rule 48](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter6/rule48_v1.00.html), [Notification 10/2023-CT](https://www.gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-05/10ct_eng.pdf), and the [dated source register](../../docs/gst-statutory-workflows-2026-11-18.md#irp-priority-2-effective-dates-and-source-register) for amendments and supplier exceptions.
- **Reporting limit, not mandate:** [GSTN advisory 543](https://www.gst.gov.in/newsandupdates/read/543) applies a 30-day reporting restriction from **1 April 2025** at **₹10 crore and above** for IRN-required invoices and credit/debit notes. Day 30 is inclusive in India. The chosen provider’s exact time and response contract still requires validation.
- **Local execution scope:** direct domestic Regular GST services with a saved registered recipient, supported place of supply, and the required issued particulars. No export or SEZ wire format, other special supplies, historical reinterpretation, automatic tax treatment or filing. A mandate can apply even when AutoDine does not support the route.

## What shipped locally

| Workflow | Implemented boundary |
|---|---|
| Prepare from saved documents | An entitled outlet Owner can validate the held invoice, credit note or debit note offline. INV-01 uses the issued registration, party particulars, India dates and numbers, service SAC, reference to the original, and exact saved paise. An unknown or unsuitable source is not a sendable request. No amount or counter is recalculated. |
| Select route and queue | On explicitly opted-in loopback only, Owner chooses a compatible, provisioned simulation route and queues the immutable preparation. Manager reads only. No arbitrary endpoint or signer entry, global admin button, automatic job runner, or route installed by the migrations. Identity and route pins do not change through owner requests. |
| Claim, verify and recover | Private SQL worker uses a separately committed lease, persistent rate slot, token/revision checks and scoped provider/version/key. Both signed test bodies must match saved document identity and particulars, exact amounts, SAC, acknowledgement and QR. Timeout/duplicate/mismatched signature or expired claim requires lookup, not resend. A new owner-requested send is bounded and requires affirmative proof of no registration or no send. The redacted event history is append-only. |
| Request cancellation | Separate reason and audit, issued source and write entitlement, exact 24-hour acknowledgement window, no unresolved linked adjustments on original invoice cancellation, and known no active e-way bill. A lost confirmation blocks active copy until lookup. Cancellation does not void locally, reuse numbers, change tax or payment, or issue a note. |
| Register and private output | `/dashboard/reports/irp` shows held, prepared and dispatched work, deadlines, route availability, scoped provider outcomes and audit; a filtered no-store CSV omits GSTINs, requests and signed material. Owner/Manager reads are selected-outlet only. Only a trusted active **authority** projection could supply the private printable IRN/ACK/exact signed QR. There is no writer or active authority route in this build. Simulation and bare `generated`/legacy `irn` cannot release a held source or enable a bearer link or receipt text. |
